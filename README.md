# Corporate Profitability & Working Capital Dashboard

An executive financial model and analytics dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to evaluate commercial revenue streams, cost of goods sold (COGS), gross margins, and customer payment statuses across operating sectors.

---

## 💼 Business Problem
Corporate finance teams require real-time visibility into product profitability and receivable aging to protect operating liquidity. This project models transactional sales data across key industry verticals to evaluate margin performance and isolate working capital risks.

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics Analyzed
- **Total Revenue:** $122,600.00 in gross billings generated across all customer accounts.
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** Direct fulfillment expenses across product lines ($76,100.00).
- **Gross Profit & Margin %:** Unit economics evaluation achieving a blended 38% gross margin ($46,500.00 profit).
- **Sector Breakdown:** Revenue distribution analyzed across Technology ($45,500), Retail ($24,100), and Manufacturing ($53,000).
- **Working Capital Status:** Identifies paid vs. unpaid accounts to track liquidity and receivables.

---

## 🛠️ Excel Skills & Functions Demonstrated
- **Formulas & Functions:** Conditional logic (`IF`), aggregations (`SUM`, `SUMIF`), and dynamic ratio calculations.
- **Data Modeling:** Multi-tier architectural design across structured tabs (`Raw_Data`, `Calculations`, `Dashboard`).
- **Data Visualization:** Executive KPI cards, custom-formatted clustered column charts, and clean layout design without gridlines.
